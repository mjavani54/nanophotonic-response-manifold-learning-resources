# Validation performed for this package

The new-run source generator SHA-256 matches the recorded material manifest. The source design NPZ SHA-256 also matches that manifest. The original augmented source archive available for inspection matched its recorded SHA-256 exactly; design-only arrays were extracted from it without historical response labels. The earlier 20-layer source `designs.npy` matched its historical manifest SHA-256 and was value-for-value identical to the corresponding source design CSV.

The rebuilt primary 50k ZIP has a different container SHA-256 from the run metadata, but all six archived L14/L16/L18 design and response CSV member hashes match the new run's recorded hashes. For all seven 50k classes, reconstructed response minima and maxima equal the recorded `intrinsic/qc_summary.csv` values. The separate 200k 20-layer reconstructed design and response minima/maxima equal `extended_20/learning_curve_qc.json` values. These extrema alone are not an array-identity proof.

All three reconstructed augmented NPZs match their new-run file SHA-256 values exactly. Reconstructing them again from the compact design-only NPZ with `scripts/reproduce_augmentation.py` also reproduces those exact file hashes. Four spectral-window response arrays were calculated from the two verified 20-layer source-design ensembles. Their PCA99 counts and all twelve reported held-out PCA R² diagnostic values match the recorded JSON exactly. `scripts/reproduce_spectral.py` reproduces the asset hashes. The reconstructed B-wide response matches the corresponding reconstructed primary L20 CSV when loaded as float32.

`scripts/verify_package.py` checks the repository checksum manifest, 352 individual files inside the recorded-fit archive, the present data-asset manifest, the six primary member hashes, and the three augmented exact hashes. `verify_summary.py` checks the 72-row primary learning grid, first-tested thresholds, six resolved capacity endpoints, 48 spectral fit rows, and augmented and separate 20-layer summaries. Article Tables I and II were independently compared cell-for-cell with the attached working manuscript, and the figures are the six new-run figures from its working package.

**Unresolved identity boundary:** The original two generated primary/200k ZIP containers were not supplied. Their rebuilt ZIP hashes differ from the original recorded hashes, so whole-container identity cannot be claimed. The original byte hashes of every individual primary/200k response member and of the four spectral response arrays were not recorded in the material available. The neural training fits were not rerun on the reconstructed assets here. Do not claim that the full run was independently reproduced or that every reconstructed array is byte-identical to the original input.
