# Figure provenance

`Figure1`, `Figure3`, `Figure4`, and `Figure5` are descriptive plots drawn from
`../results/final_master_summary.csv` with `python scripts/plot_summary_figures.py`.
They show operational reconstruction thresholds and first tested resource
crossings in one related multilayer family. The two smallest training-size
thresholds are left-censored at 1,000; the 20-layer fixed-50k capacity
threshold is unresolved and excluded from the capacity plot.

The controlled learning curves and extended 20-layer curve are frozen results
from the analysis scripts. The revised Table of Contents image is an original
schematic and does not assert a causal or cross-family prediction.
